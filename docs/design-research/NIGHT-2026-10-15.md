## In one paragraph

Both explorations now **render directly from PostGIS** as new `srk-render` presets — `--layers la` (landscape-architecture plan) and `--layers ink` (fantasy-book ink map). No image post-processing: every crown, hatch line, label and shadow comes from OSM data. Tiles are seamless (checked: metatile 1 vs 4 differ in < 0.1 % of pixels). The whole Schillerkiez (974 tiles, z16–19) renders in 16–40 s (LA) / 29–65 s (ink) with 6 workers, depending on how busy the machine is. 18–19 iterations per style are documented as step folders with images and notes, plus variants for the open design questions, printed map sheets and an interactive viewer.

## Decisions taken during the night

1. **Renderer: Rust.** Tobias left it to me ("du musst das Design ja anschauen, bewerten, iterieren"). Rust gives stroke-level control and a 2–3 s render → look → change loop for the scenes. MapLibre was ruled out (pixel effects), QGIS would have been slower to iterate headless.
2. **Database:** rebuilt in the repo's Docker stack (`strassenraumkarte-db-1`, port 5433) for bbox `13.4102,52.4680,13.4520,52.4832` (Schillerkiez → Richardplatz/Körnerpark) — 1.5 min import, < 100 MB. The TILDA containers on port 5432 were not touched.
3. **Ink houses: plan view by default** (Tobias: "muss nicht 3D sein … Häuserreihen sehr schwer"). Volume comes from slope hatching, ridge, a cross-hatched shaded roof half and a narrow south facade band. Landmarks (church) and trees stand upright. **Plus a variant** `SRK_INK_OBLIQUE=freestanding` that lifts only free-standing buildings into an oblique view — I think it should become the default (see open questions).
4. **Accent is a parameter:** `SRK_ACCENT=playground|bench|tree|none`.
5. **LA palette from Tobias's board** (the swatch pin), cooler and greyer than the first mockup; a `vivid` variant for the saturated look of modern competition plans.
6. Two **extra scenes** (E Richardplatz, F Körnerpark) — village houses for the oblique question, a formal garden for the LA style.

## Numbers

- Render time, Schillerkiez z16–19, 974 tiles, 6 workers, WebP: full-approx 10.5–26 ms/tile · LA 16.6–41 ms/tile · ink 29–66 ms/tile. The higher values were measured while a separate `streetlevel-ortho` Python pipeline kept the CPU busy.
- Seams (`tools/seam_check.sh`, metatile 1 vs 4): LA 0.065 %, ink 0.03–0.09 % of pixels differ by more than 8/255 (anti-aliasing only).
- Repo: committed design-research files ≈ 40 MB, mostly step sheets (ink sheets compress poorly, ~0.6 MB each); later steps are saved smaller. Reference screenshots stay local (gitignored).
