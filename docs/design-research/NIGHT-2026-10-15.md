Branch `claude/design-styles-rust` (based on `experiment-rust-pixel-port-vector-map`, with the design-research commits cherry-picked on top).

## In one paragraph

Both explorations now **render directly from PostGIS** as new `srk-render` presets — `--layers la` (landscape-architecture plan) and `--layers ink` (fantasy-book ink map). No image post-processing: every crown, hatch line, label and shadow comes from OSM data. Tiles are seamless (checked: metatile 1 vs 4 differ in < 0.1 % of pixels), the whole Schillerkiez (974 tiles, z16–19) renders in ~16 s (LA) / ~29 s (ink) with 6 workers. Iterations are documented as step folders with images and notes.

## Decisions taken during the night

1. **Renderer: Rust.** Tobias left it to me ("du musst das Design ja anschauen, bewerten, iterieren"). Rust gives stroke-level control and a 2–3 s render → look → change loop for the four scenes. MapLibre was ruled out (pixel effects), QGIS would have been slower to iterate headless.
2. **Database:** rebuilt in the repo's Docker stack (`strassenraumkarte-db-1`, port 5433) for the Schillerkiez bbox `13.4102,52.4707,13.4370,52.4832` only — 1.5 min import, 43 MB. The TILDA containers on port 5432 were not touched.
3. **Ink houses: plan view, no oblique 3D** (Tobias: "muss nicht 3D sein … Häuserreihen sehr schwer"). Volume comes from slope hatching, ridge, a cross-hatched shaded roof half and a narrow south facade band. Landmarks (church) and trees stand upright.
4. **Accent is a parameter:** `SRK_ACCENT=playground|bench|tree|none`.
5. **LA palette from Tobias's board** (the swatch pin), cooler and greyer than the first mockup.

## Numbers

- Render time, Schillerkiez z16–19, 974 tiles, 6 workers, WebP: full-approx 10.5 ms/tile · LA 16.6 ms/tile · ink 29 ms/tile (another CPU-heavy process was running on the machine).
- Seams (`tools/seam_check.sh`, metatile 1 vs 4): LA 0.065 %, ink 0.086 % of pixels differ by more than 8/255 (anti-aliasing).
- Repo: committed design-research files ≈ 15–20 MB (reference screenshots stay local).

## System during the night

Logged every 5 minutes (CPU idle, load, memory, free disk). Disk stayed at 11–13 GB free, memory pressure never critical; the main CPU load came from a separate `streetlevel-ortho` Python pipeline that was already running.
