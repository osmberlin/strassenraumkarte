# Ink 02 — one spire per landmark, parts as lines

- `building:part` polygons no longer get their own roof hatch and facade band; they are thin lines on the roof.
- Landmarks (`church`, `chapel`, `cathedral`) get exactly one spire, placed on their **smallest** polygon — at Herrfurthplatz that is the tower of the Genezarethkirche.
