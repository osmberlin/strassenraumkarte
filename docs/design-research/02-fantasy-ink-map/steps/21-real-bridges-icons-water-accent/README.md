# Ink 21 — real bridges only, larger landmark icons, water accent

- Bridges are drawn only where they cross something: rails, water, or a lower road under a deck of at least 100 m². The many small `bridge` polygons over courtyard ramps and light wells (scene D) stay plain.
- Bell turrets (schools) and clock towers (civic buildings) are about 1.5× larger — at the first size they read as specks.
- `SRK_ACCENT=water` shown at z18: fountains, pumps and drinking water in vermilion around Körnerpark.
