# LA 04 — zoom-stable textures, muted markings, play equipment

- Scatter textures (shrub dabs) use a zoom-scaled world cell (`zcell`), so on-screen density is the same at z17–z20 and still seamless across tiles.
- Road markings come from the shared painters but are drawn into a separate layer and pulled 80 % towards grey — no signal red in a site plan.
- Play equipment: structures white with a thin outline, sandpits pale sand, slides/beams as dark strokes, nodes as small rings (z18+).

**Look at:** `zooms.jpg` (scene A at z17 and z18), `detail-d.jpg` (playground).
