# LA 08 — spherical crowns, pitch lines

- Crown variants "disc" and "scalloped" are filled with a radial gradient lit from the north-west (light cap → base → slightly darker edge): crowns read as volumes, like the rendered plans on the board.
- Pitch markings are drawn procedurally from `pitch_markings` (centre, Mercator-metre extents, direction): outline, centre line, centre circle, goal areas / keys — white on the pitch green. No SVG stamps needed.
